It's true:

For the first time in three years, we have new music for you.

Download or stream "She Moved to Oklahoma" now from our [Bandcamp](http://thedeafeningcolors.bandcamp.com) site.


![](/img/covers/she-moved-to-oklahoma.jpg)

On Sunday, it should be up on additional listening platforms (Spotify, Apple Music, etc.).

As always, if you like what you hear, please tell your friends. A mention, or better, a link to the actual song on your preferred social media platform goes a long way toward helping us to make more music.

Stay tuned. There's much more to come...
