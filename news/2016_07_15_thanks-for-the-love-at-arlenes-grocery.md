Thanks to all who came to see us the other night at Arlene's Grocery in NYC!

It was a blast - they always have great sound, a good vibe, and nice people. It didn't hurt that we were playing alongside [The Danbees](http://www.thedanbees.com), who we love to play alongside. Cris from TDC is Danbees front-man Mark's brother, so shows with the two of us are always a fun family affair.

When are we playing next? We'll have a few words on just that in the very, very near future. Stay tuned!
