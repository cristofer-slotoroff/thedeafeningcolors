We are pleased to announce that [RUN PASS OPTION](http://thedeafeningcolors.bandcamp.com/) our first record since 2015, is available for streaming and downloading right now.

We have embedded the Bandcamp player below, but these are some of the links for streaming, listening, and purchasing information:

[Bandcamp](http://thedeafeningcolors.bandcamp.com/)

[Spotify](http://open.spotify.com/artist/46PyW6iCYaZPO5Ua83ibKf)
