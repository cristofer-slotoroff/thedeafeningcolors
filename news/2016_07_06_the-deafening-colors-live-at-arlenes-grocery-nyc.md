Come see us at Arlene's Grocery!

This will be our only NYC show this summer and our second in support of NYC's own rock and roll force of nature, The Danbees! Here's what you need to know:

Who? The Deafening Colors and The Danbees.

Where? Arlene's Grocery: 95 Stanton Street New York City, NY 10002

When? Wednesday, July 13th. 9PM sharp. Don't be late!

How Much? Tickets are $8 in advance, and they may sell out at the door as it's a pretty tight venue. Get yours in advance!

How do I get tickets? Easy: [get tickets here](http://www.ticketfly.com/purchase/event/1215401?_ga=1.220988056.1483547780.1464311212&__utma=1.2078578880.1464311212.1467163285.1467855318.3&__utmb=1.2.10.1467855318&__utmc=1&__utmx=-&__utmz=1.1467855318.3.3.utmcsr=google|utmccn=(organic)|utmcmd=organic|utmctr=(not%20provided)&__utmv=-&__utmk=71780243), or check the website for [Arlene's Grocery](http://www.arlenesgrocery.net), [The Deafening Colors](http://www.thedeafeningcolors.com), or [The Danbees ](http://www.thedanbees.com)if you'd like more information.

Arlene's Grocery is a small space, too, so get your tickets early! We expect to sell it out!
