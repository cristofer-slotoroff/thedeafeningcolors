After a brief hiatus, we mark our live return this Friday at The Delancey. We're kicking things off at 8PM, but be sure to stick around for Terra and the Dactyls, One Day Steady, The Manimals, and the late night DJ set that is to follow.

We're on at 8PM and it's only a $10 cover at the door. See you there!

The Facebook event page is attached here, so RSVP, share it, and most importantly: show up and enjoy some great live music!

https://www.facebook.com/events/185341218536355/
